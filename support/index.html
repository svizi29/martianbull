Support

Help with our games, straight from the people who made them.

Current status: Ave Morituri (working title) is in development and has not been released. There is no public build to download yet, and nothing on any store is ours. When that changes, this page will say so.

Getting help

Email contact@martianbull.example and a human — one of the three of us — will read it. We aim to reply within a few working days.

If you are reporting a problem, these four things let us fix it much faster:

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 14)
  • OS version (e.g. iOS 19.2)
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one

Common questions

When is the game out?

When it’s ready. We have not announced a release date, and any date you see elsewhere is not from us.

Which platforms?

iOS first, in landscape. Android is planned to follow. No other platforms are announced.

Is it free? What’s the catch?

Free to play. The only things we ever intend to sell are cosmetics — identity and status. The debt at the heart of the game can never be paid down with real money, there is no pay-to-win, and there are no loot boxes. That’s the whole catch.

Can I beta test?

There is no public test yet. If you’d like to be considered when one opens, email us with your device model and we’ll keep a list.

Refunds and purchases

Nothing is for sale yet. Once the game is live, purchases will be handled by Apple’s App Store or Google Play, and refunds go through them — we never see or store your payment details. If a purchase misbehaves in-game, email us and we’ll sort it out.

Accounts and data deletion

The game currently has no accounts and collects no personal data, so there is nothing to delete. See the privacy policy for the full, honest picture.
