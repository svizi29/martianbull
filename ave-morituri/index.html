The hook

You owe them everything

Opening balance

−1,000,000 denarii

Payable in full before you leave the building.

Every gladiator in the arena is there for the same reason: the ledger. You start one million denarii down. A good match pays off around nine thousand. Do the arithmetic — that’s roughly a hundred and eleven matches between you and the gate — then stop doing arithmetic, because the man across the arena did his too.

Performance buys freedom. Nothing else does. You cannot pay real money to reduce the debt — not because we haven’t thought of it, but because buying your way out would break the only promise this game makes. You fight your way out, or you stay.

The show

Televised. Sponsored. You’re the content.

Seven-minute matches

One arena, seven minutes, everything on the line. Short enough for a commute, long enough to matter — and every match moves the ledger, win or lose.

The crowd

Three hundred and sixty degrees of people who paid to watch you bleed. They cheer your kills, groan your deaths, and the broadcast — announcers, sponsors, ad breaks — loves them for it.

The ledger

Your debt is real, public, and falling — if you’re good. Watch the number drop after every match. New arenas unlock as it comes down: the Empire rewards a debtor in good standing.

Bolts & heraldry

Your plasma bolts, your colours, your crest on the broadcast. Cosmetics are identity, and identity is the only thing for sale here. Power isn’t. Freedom isn’t.

For press

The short version: a gladiator game that happens to be a shooter — never the reverse. A satire about people made to die for entertainment, built by a three-person, openly AI-native studio. We’re happy to talk about the game, the debt, or the pipeline — especially the pipeline.

  • Developer: Martian Bull — three people, openly AI-native
  • Format: mobile arena PvP shooter, landscape, iOS first
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
