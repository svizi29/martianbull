The ledger · Tabulae

Every bout moves the ledger.

You are property. The arena is your only way out. After every bout the house ranks all six fighters by kill/death ratio: the sharpest blade clears the most debt, and the bottom three earn nothing. Clear enough lifetime debt and new arenas unlock: the medieval quarter, the gothic temple, the dark forest, the Greek isle, old Japan.

Custom bolt colours, a coat of arms on your armour, and a crowd that knows your name when you are winning. All of it running at a locked 30 fps on an iPhone.
