Who we are

Three people and a machine shop

Martian Bull is a three-person studio, and we are openly AI-native. AI runs through our pipeline end to end — code, art, video, audio — because that is the only way three people ship a game of this size. We would rather tell you that on the front page than have you find out later.

The machines do not decide what the game is. We do. What they buy us is the ability to build something that could not otherwise exist at this headcount — and the obligation to be honest about how it was made. So: stated up front, here, always.

How we work

Three rules we don’t break

Say it up front

We are AI-native and we say so before you ask. No stealth, no fine print, nothing to get caught doing. The pipeline is part of the story, not a secret.

Never sell freedom

In our game you fight your way out of debt. That debt can never be paid down with real money — we sell identity and status, never freedom or power. No pay-to-win, no loot boxes, no energy meters.

The joke has teeth

We make satire about people made to die for entertainment — televised, sponsored, and cheered. If that sits a little uncomfortably, it’s working.

Our first game

Ave · Moritvri

Fight Free. A Roman PvP shooter.

A gladiator game that happens to be a shooter — never the reverse. You start one million denarii in debt to the arena, and the only way out is through seven-minute matches in front of a crowd that paid to watch you bleed. Performance buys freedom. Nothing else does.
