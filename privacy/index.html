Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026

The short version: the game currently collects no personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that phone home. Your progress is saved on your own device and goes nowhere. If that ever changes — and before any analytics or advertising SDK ships — this policy will be updated first, so that it always describes what the shipped game actually does.

1. Who we are

Martian Bull (“we”, “us”) is a small game studio and the developer of Ave Morituri (working title). For anything in this policy, contact contact@martianbull.example.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers our games (currently Ave Morituri, working title) and this website. It describes what data is collected, why, and what your choices are. We have written it to be read, not skimmed past — it is short because the honest answer is short.

3. What the game collects today: nothing personal

  • No accounts. The game does not ask you to sign up, sign in, or identify yourself.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs. None are integrated. The game does not profile you, track you across apps, or show ads.
  • Local save data only. Your progress (including your in-game debt — sorry, that one’s permanent until you earn it down) is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. This website

This is a static site. We set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical request data such as IP addresses for security and operational purposes under its own privacy policy; we do not receive or use such logs to identify visitors.

5. What will change before certain features ship

We plan features that will involve some data processing. Each will be reflected here before it ships:

  • Online multiplayer. Running a live match requires transient technical data (such as your IP address and connection quality) to connect players and prevent cheating. This data would be used to operate the match, not to profile you.
  • Purchases. Any future purchases will be processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Analytics or advertising. If we integrate an analytics or advertising SDK, this policy will be updated first to name the provider and the data involved, and we will ask for consent where the law requires it.

6. Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Age ratings will be set through the stores’ own rating systems at release.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we currently hold no personal data about players, there is typically nothing for us to produce or delete — but you are always welcome to ask, and we will answer honestly: contact@martianbull.example.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top and, for material changes affecting a released game, say so in the game or its store listing. The version published here is always the current one.

9. Contact

Questions, concerns, requests: contact@martianbull.example
